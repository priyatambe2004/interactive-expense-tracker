# 💰 SpendWise – Smart Expense Tracker

SpendWise is a modern and responsive expense-tracking web application designed to help users manage their daily spending, monitor budgets, and understand their financial habits.

With a clean dashboard, interactive expense management, and useful financial insights, SpendWise makes tracking expenses simple and organized.

## ✨ Features

- **Dashboard Overview** – View total expenses, monthly spending, and average expenses.
- **Add Expenses** – Record expenses with a title, amount, category, and date.
- **Monthly Budget** – Set a monthly budget and monitor your spending progress.
- **Category Breakdown** – Organize expenses into categories for better tracking.
- **Transaction History** – View, search, and filter recorded transactions.
- **Monthly Navigation** – Explore expenses from different months.
- **Charts & Insights** – Understand spending patterns through visual summaries.
- **CSV Export** – Export expense records for offline use.
- **Data Backup & Import** – Back up and restore your expense data.
- **Dark Mode** – Switch between light and dark themes.
- **Local Storage** – Keep your expense data saved in your browser.
- **Responsive Design** – Works across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Browser Local Storage
